TASK A — CCF SPEC GAP CLOSURE
------------------------------
Status: SELECTED

CCF Governing PDE (adopted, to appear in ANCHORS amendment):
  omega_t + u dot omega_x = u_x dot omega, u_x = H[omega]

  This is the a=0 member of the gCLM family already defined in
  ANCHORS.md line 12 (equation: omega_t + a.u.omega_x = u_x.omega,
  u_x = Homega; c_omega = -1 fixed). At a=0 the advection term
  a.u.omega_x drops, giving omega_t = (Homega).omega.

  Source: Constantin-Lax-Majda (1985), Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 38, 715,
  Eq. (2.1)-(2.3); Wang et al. (2025), arXiv:2509.14185, Eqs. (1)-(3).

Self-similar profile ODE:
  c_l.Omega + alpha.xi.Omega' - HOmega.Omega = 0
  (this is the a=0 case of the gCLM profile ODE already implemented
   in engine/f1.py compute_profile_ode_rhs(Omega, a=0.0, c_l, alpha))

Linearized operator (whose eigenvalues are the published lambda values):
  L[f] = c_l.f + alpha.xi.f' - HOmega.f - Hf.Omega

SELECTION RATIONALE (verifiable-before-any-number):
  The CCF (Constantin-Lax-Majda) equation is uniquely identified by
  its name and its place in the gCLM family hierarchy. The family
  is parameterised by a in the PDE:
    omega_t + a.u.omega_x = u_x.omega, u_x = H[omega]
  The CCF is the a=0 member. This identification is determined by
  the source literature (Constantin-Lax-Majda 1985 derive a=0 form;
  Wang 2509.14185 credits that derivation) and by the exclusion of
  other a-values whose physical interpretation differs (a=0 = pure
  vortex stretching; a=1 = De Gregorio advection + stretching; a<0
  = degenerate half-line). No eigenvalue targets are consulted in
  this selection. The rationale is identical under any substitution
  of the published lambda values.

REJECTED ALTERNATIVE FORMS:
  1. gCLM with a != 0 (e.g. a=1/2, a=1, a<0):
     Rejected because the published lambda values are from CCF
     literature (Constantin-Lax-Majda, Wang), not from De Gregorio
     or mixed-family profiles. Attributing CCF lambda to a!=0 would
     be a misattribution of the source.
  2. Dissipative/viscous CCF (with nu.omega_xx term):
     Rejected because Wang (2509.14185, 2511.22819) and
     Constantin-Lax-Majda (1985) study the inviscid equation.
     The lambda values are eigenvalues of the inviscid operator.
  3. 2D/3D surface quasi-geostrophic form:
     Rejected because project scope is 1D (SPEC sec 1).
