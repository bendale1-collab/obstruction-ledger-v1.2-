TASK 3 — P1 SCOPE ADJUDICATION
--------------------------------
Recommended: P1-SCOPE-OK with the following pre-registered constraints:

Q1: Is Fourier spectral on [-L,L] adequate to resolve the
    spectrum of L_0?
    YES, with caveats:
    - Xu (2607.19762) proves the essential spectrum is Re λ = -1/2
      line. Our Fourier discretization on [-L, L] will approximate
      this as a cluster near -0.5. The essential spectrum is
      continuous — any finite discretization produces a discrete
      approximation, and eigenvalues near the essential spectrum
      may be artifacts.
    - The Hilbert transform is exact under Fourier (multiplier
      -i·sgn(k)). The remaining operations (differentiation,
      multiplication by the potential) are also exact under Fourier.
      No Fourier-series reduction is blocked — the operator L_0 is
      Fourier-diagonalizable in its principal part.
    - WHAT OUR DISCRETIZATION COMPUTES: the eigenvalues of the
      matrix L_N approximating L_0 on the subspace of Fourier modes
      |k| ≤ N. The essential spectrum is approximated by the
      eigenvalues near Re λ = -0.5; eigenvalues with Re λ > -0.5
      (excluding 0,1) that survive resolution doubling are candidate
      unstable eigenvalues of the continuation branch.

Q2: Can deflation distinguish a true unstable mode from a
    discretization artifact in the presence of continuous essential
    spectrum?
    NOT by itself. Deflation removes a known root from Newton's
    method but does not distinguish eigenvalue types. The required
    diagnostic is:
    - The eigenvalue must have Re λ > -0.5 + δ (δ = some margin
      above the essential spectrum line). Eigenvalues with Re λ
      within 0.01 of -0.5 are ESSENTIAL-SPECTRUM ARTIFACTS and
      must be rejected.
    - The eigenmode must be square-integrable (decay at infinity).
    - Resolution doubling: λ(N) vs λ(2N) must agree to at least
      4 digits.
    - The mode must not be 0 or 1 (symmetry modes; removed by
      modulation).

Q3: Under strong non-normality, conditioning of a 13-digit
    eigenvalue match?
    Xu (2607.19762) explicitly flags non-normality: L_0 has
    pseudospectra that can make eigenvalue counts misleading.
    A 13-digit match to a published value is NOT trustworthy
    without a pseudospectral check:
    - Pre-registered precondition: for any eigenvalue λ claimed
      as a P1 hit, compute the resolvent norm ||(L - λI)^{-1}||
      or the ε-pseudospectrum. If the resolvent is large
      (> 10^3) within ε < 10^{-3} of λ, the eigenvalue is
      non-normal and its accuracy cannot be trusted to more
      than ~3 digits regardless of the discrete residual.
    - The ε-pseudospectrum can be estimated via the singular
      value decomposition of (L - λI) at points near λ.
