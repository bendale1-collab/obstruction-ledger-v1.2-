TASK 1 — CORRECT CCF EQUATION (verified from primary sources)
--------------------------------------------------------------
CCF = Córdoba-Córdoba-Fontelos (2005), Ann. Math. 162, 1377–1389,
"Formation of singularities for a transport equation with nonlocal
velocity." Verified from PDF of Annals paper (Córdoba-Córdoba-Fontelos):

  θ_t − H[θ]·θ_x = 0              (inviscid, Eq 1.1)
  θ_t − H[θ]·θ_x = −νΛ^α θ        (viscous, Eq 1.1)

where H is the Hilbert transform and Λ = (−∂_xx)^{1/2}.

No stretching term (u_x·ω) appears. The nonlinearity is advective:
the scalar θ is transported by its own Hilbert transform as velocity.
Córdoba et al. explicitly distinguish this from CLM (Eq 1.3 in their
paper): "ω_t + v·ω_x = ωHω; this is the Constantin–Lax–Majda model."
CCF ≠ CLM.

Wang 2509.14185 uses the SAME CCF equation. Verified from PDF:
  Inviscid CCF: θ_t − H[θ]·θ_x = 0 (same as Córdoba)
  Self-similar ansatz with λ: φ(x,t) = (1−t)^{kA(λ)} Φ_A(y)
    y = (1−t)^{-(1+λ)} x
  The self-similar parameter λ is the BLOW-UP SCALING RATE
  (not an eigenvalue of any linearized operator).
  λ governs how quickly the singularity's spatial support shrinks.

Lambda values in Wang 2509.14185 table (verified from PDF):
  Stable:   λ₀ = 1.1807776628998    (13-digit, EXACT match to ANCHORS)
  1st:      λ₁ = 0.6057337012032    (13-digit, EXACT match to ANCHORS)
  2nd:      λ₂ = 0.4713248638620    (table value; text says 0.4703)
  3rd:      λ₃ = 0.2415604743989    (not in ANCHORS)
  4th:      not reported for CCF

Lambda values in Wang 2511.22819 (refinement):
  2nd unstable: λₛ = 0.47132422      (8-digit, gradient-normalized)
  Comparison shows λ = 0.4713241 with |λ−λₛ| ~ 10⁻⁷

The 0.4713242245 in our ANCHORS matches neither the 2509 table value
(0.4713248638620, differing at 7th digit) nor the 2511.22819 refined
value (0.47132422). It appears to be a conflation or truncation — the
source is unclear.

Is CCF a member of gCLM/OSW?
  NO. The gCLM/OSW family is:
    ω_t + a·u·ω_x = u_x·ω,  u_x = Hω
  CCF: θ_t = H[θ]·θ_x  (transport of θ, no stretching term ω·u_x)
  Structural difference: CCF nonlinearity involves θ_x (derivative);
  gCLM nonlinearity involves ω (multiplicative). Different PDE classes.

Consequence: the gCLM a=0 (= CLM, Constantin-Lax-Majda) is a
DIFFERENT equation from CCF. Our F1 engine was built for gCLM a=0,
which matches the CLM spectral theory (Xu 2607.19762) and the
Elgindi-Jeong exact profiles, but does NOT match CCF. The λ values
in ANCHORS are CCF blow-up scaling rates, not gCLM eigenvalues.
