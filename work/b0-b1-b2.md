# B1 — H2 REALIZATION PRE-REGISTRATION

## Generalized eigenproblem

L_odd @ v = λ * M @ v

where L_odd = P L_full P (the odd-projected CLM linearized operator on
[−L,L]) and M is the H²(0,∞) inner-product matrix on the same grid.

## Derivation of M from Xu Eq 3.2

Xu Eq 3.2 defines:
> X = { φ : φ odd, φ, φ'' ∈ L²(0,∞), φ(y) = a₁y + o(y) as y → 0 (a₁ ∈ ℂ) }

The Hilbertian norm on X is:
> ‖φ‖²_X = ‖φ‖²_L² + ‖φ''‖²_L²

So the inner product is:
> ⟨φ, ψ⟩_X = ∫ φ ψ dy + ∫ φ'' ψ'' dy

On the Fourier grid [−L, L] with odd extension, the discrete form of M:

M = I + D² · D²

where:
- I = identity matrix (approximates ⟨φ, ψ⟩_L²)
- D² = second-derivative matrix (spectral Fourier, via FFT)
- M = I + (D²)ᵀ · D² ≈ I + D⁴ (the biharmonic, approximating the H² seminorm)

**Discrete form:** M = I + (D²)^T · D² where D² is the spectral second
derivative matrix on the full grid (not the half-domain), then odd-projected:
M_odd = P M P, restricted to right_ix.

The generalized eigenproblem is:
  L_odd @ v = λ * M_odd @ v

where L_odd and M_odd are both (M/2 × M/2) real (or complex) matrices.
